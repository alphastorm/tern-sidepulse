# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-10

- **Breaking requirement — SidePulse with `program_status` ([inteliwear/sidepulse#72](https://github.com/inteliwear/sidepulse/pull/72))**: Requires a SidePulse build installed or updated with `sidepulse update` on or after 2026-11-10 (merge commit `ad4a5217`). When an older SidePulse is present (`relay-events.sock` without `program-status.sock`), `tern-sidepulse: doctor` reports an error on `receiver` (`this SidePulse predates program status`) with the remedy `sidepulse update`. Use `tern-sidepulse` `0.1.0` for older SidePulse builds.
- SidePulse program-status transport (`tern_sidepulse/transport.py`, `tern_sidepulse/coordinator.py`, `tern_sidepulse/doctor.py`): replaces the 0.1.0 hook-event transport (`events.sock`, `relay-events.sock`, `UserPromptSubmit`/`Interrupt`/… hook-event lines, and the single combined `tern-sidepulse:<install_id>` session) with `SidePulseTransport` over SidePulse's `program-status.sock` (`$XDG_STATE_HOME/sidepulse/agent-monitor/program-status.sock`, `~/.local/state/sidepulse/agent-monitor/program-status.sock`, or `/tmp/sidepulse-status-<uid>/<sha256(path)[:16]>.sock` when the `program-status.sock` path exceeds 96 bytes). Each OMP session is reported as its own row under salted scope `tern-sidepulse-<24 hex>` (`sha256(install_id NUL host NUL pane)[:24]`, salted by `install_id` from `identity.json`), mapped to states `working`, `blocked` (`waiting_for_input`), `error` (`blocked_error`), `done` (`completed`), `idle` (`idle_ready`), and `clear` across at most 32 rows (most urgent first). Unchanged rows are not resent; active rows (`working`, `blocked`, `error`) refresh every 300 s; retries reuse `request_id` with 2, 4, 8, 16, 30 s backoff and wake immediately when a socket appears or changes; SidePulse restarts (including a move to another socket candidate) resubmit every current row except delivered `done` rows; and a scope ledger (`<runtime dir>/program-scopes.json`, mode `0600`, salted scope hashes only) written before each scope's first report lets the next coordinator overwrite still-open panes in place and clear stale ones after a 15 s grace period following a crash (at once if they would exceed the 32-row cap). An exiting coordinator keeps clearing rows for up to 2 s, and `disable` now stops the coordinator after clearing its rows, so the coordinator that `enable` lets the next snapshot start reads `config.json` afresh.
- Per-pane rows and session labels (`window.luau`, `tern_sidepulse/coordinator.py`, `tern_sidepulse/reducer.py`, `tests/test_privacy.py`, `tests/test_rows.py`, `tests/test_transport.py`): observations are keyed by `(host, pane)` (`local`, or a remote host's `address`, `name`, or `id:<n>`) and deduplicated across windows by mode priority (`blocked_error`, `waiting_for_input`, `working`, `completed`, `idle_ready`) and newest non-empty label. When a window has at least one OMP pane, `window.luau` reads `cx.session:panes()` and `cx.session:tabs()` at most once per 5 s pass and labels each OMP pane from its user-given tab name (`TabInfo.name`) or its own `PaneInfo.title` (cut to 256 UTF-8 bytes in the window and sanitized in the coordinator to at most 96 characters without control, surrogate, or private-use characters). Sanitized labels are sent to SidePulse in `context.title` by default (`"labels": false` in `<plugin data dir>/config.json` sends `"title": ""` and `"display_name": "OMP in Tern"` instead); host keys never appear outside coordinator memory, pane ids and host keys never leave the Mac (SidePulse reports and `program-scopes.json` carry salted scope hashes only), and labels never appear in `coordinator.log`, `doctor.json`, `bridge.py status`, coordinator replies, or `program-scopes.json`.
- Configuration, environment, CLI, and status updates (`tern_sidepulse/cli.py`, `tern_sidepulse/client.py`, `tern_sidepulse/doctor.py`): `<plugin data dir>/config.json` takes `program_sockets` (replacing `ui_sockets` and `service_sockets`, which are ignored if still present) and `labels` (`false` disables labels); `TERN_SIDEPULSE_PROGRAM_SOCKETS` replaces `TERN_SIDEPULSE_UI_SOCKETS` and `TERN_SIDEPULSE_SERVICE_SOCKETS`; `bridge.py coordinator --program-socket PATH` (repeatable) replaces `--ui-socket` and `--service-socket`; `doctor` replaces `receiver_ui` and `receiver_service` with `receiver` (plus `receiver_rejections` when SidePulse rejects a report) and shows `install <first 8 chars>…` on its `identity` check; and `status` drops `session_id` in favour of per-mode `rows` counts, `rows_total`, `rows_labelled`, `status.transport`, and `delivery = {"program_status": …}` in coordinator replies.
- Snapshot budget and request limits (`window.luau`, `tern_sidepulse/limits.py`): the window keeps each encoded snapshot within 192 KiB by dropping labels and retrying once (`labels_dropped`), host keys are capped at 128 bytes in the window and 128 characters in the coordinator, and the coordinator request cap is raised from 256 KiB to 1 MiB.
- Headless Luau window harness and contract checks (`tests/luau/`, `tests/test_luau_harness.py`, `tests/test_sidepulse_contract.py`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`): runs the real `window.luau` under the `luau` 0.745 CLI across the backoff/interpreter/latch/role test drive (`drive_backoff.luau`, scenarios A–G), the label and host-key drive (`drive_labels.luau`, scenarios L1–L12), and the snapshot-budget drive (`drive_budget.luau`, scenarios B1–B3), and checks the `program_status` wire contract against SidePulse `ad4a5217`.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

First release.

- Window plugin (`window.luau`) for [Tern](https://stencil.so/tern): observes the [OMP](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi) sessions visible in open Tern windows across local and attached remote hosts, classifies panes by their `omp.session` surface role, and reports aggregate status (`working`, `waiting_for_input`, `completed`, `blocked_error`, `idle_ready`) on [SidePulse](https://github.com/inteliwear/sidepulse) as one synthetic session (`tern-sidepulse:<install_id>`).
- Standard-library Python 3.10+ helper and on-demand per-user coordinator (`bridge.py`, `tern_sidepulse`): singleton lock, observer registration and 15 s leases, fixed-deadline outcome notices (15 s completion, 30 s failure), sleep-aware clock checks, bounded retry to both SidePulse receivers (`events.sock` for the menu-bar app and `relay-events.sock` for the headless service), and automatic `idle_ready` clear and exit after the last window closes.
- Tern command palette actions (`tern-sidepulse: doctor`, `tern-sidepulse: resync`, `tern-sidepulse: disable`, `tern-sidepulse: enable`) and matching `bridge.py` CLI commands (`status`, `doctor`, `resync`, `disable`, `enable`).
- Verified against Tern 0.9.12 (`e498b49`), OMP 18.16.0, and SidePulse `0.2.4.dev6+gffb1d3c2f` (revision `ffb1d3c2`) with 111 unit and integration tests; see [docs/compatibility.md](docs/compatibility.md).
- Hardening: isolated `python3 -I -S` helper launches with bytecode cached under the plugin data directory (`pycache/`), strict UTF-8 JSON validation and 256 KiB request / 32-connection caps on the coordinator socket, mode `0600` runtime and state files in a `0700` directory, and automatic rejection of pre-3.10 interpreters with exponential retry backoff up to 30 s (see commits `bf38d95` and `8446d6a`).
- Presentation: the Pane mark, banner and social preview (`assets/`), a synthetic demo animation (`docs/media/`, rendered by `scripts/render_assets.py`), the public site (`site/`, deployed by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`), the brand spec (`docs/BRAND.md`), and `tests/test_site.py`, which keeps the README, `plugin.toml`, the palette commands, and the site coherent.
