Your OMP sessions in Tern. On SidePulse.

A Tern window plugin that shows the OMP sessions in your open Tern windows on SidePulse's LEDs and menu-bar status, remote hosts included. It never reads a transcript or submits a prompt.
no network listener · no transcripts read · nothing installed on remote hosts Community project; not affiliated with or endorsed by the Oh My Pi, Tern, or SidePulse maintainers.
