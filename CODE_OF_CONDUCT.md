# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for contributors and users regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique designs, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing security and correctness directly.
- Respect privacy and never disclose another person's transcript, prompt, pane title, working directory, host or user
  name, private path, or unreviewed diagnostic data.
- Accept maintainer decisions and documented scope boundaries even when proposing alternatives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained disruption, personal attacks,
deliberate secret disclosure, and attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe publication or into claiming
compatibility the evidence does not support are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `@alphastorm` through a private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile
or in the repository's security/community settings. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions,
reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation based on severity and pattern.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project chat, release
coordination, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

Community project; not affiliated with or endorsed by the Oh My Pi, Tern, or SidePulse maintainers.
